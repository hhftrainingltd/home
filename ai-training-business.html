Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers before your business books AI training.

These FAQs are written for owners, managers and decision-makers who need to understand whether the training is suitable for their staff.

Is this suitable for staff with no AI experience?

Yes. The training can start with the basics and explain AI in plain business language. Staff do not need technical knowledge.

Can the session be tailored to our business?

Yes. Examples, scenarios and activities can be adapted to your sector, departments, staff roles and current AI priorities.

Does the training cover data and confidentiality?

Yes. Staff receive clear guidance on sensitive information, client data, commercial details, privacy risks and responsible use.

Can leaders receive a separate session?

Yes. Leadership briefings can focus on adoption, policy, risk, governance, staff expectations and implementation planning.

Can this be delivered online?

Yes. The training can be delivered in-house, live online or as a hybrid programme depending on your team location and schedule.

Is the CPD accredited?

HHF Training CPD programmes are accredited by an approved UK awarding body, giving organisations a stronger staff development record.
