Approval Readiness

Prepare the systems, documents and staff evidence awarding organisations expect to see.

HHF Training can help you organise the practical evidence behind centre approval preparation. Approval decisions remain with the relevant awarding organisation, but your centre can be better prepared, clearer and more confident.

AO

A structured preparation route for centre approval conversations.

Support can include document review, readiness checks, improvement actions, staff role clarity and practical preparation for the questions, evidence and systems normally expected during approval activity.

  • Centre overview, delivery model and qualification intentions.
  • Staff matrix, roles, responsibilities and professional development evidence.
  • Assessment, IQA, standardisation and appeals processes.
  • Malpractice, complaints, learner support and reasonable adjustment procedures.
  • Resource, venue, online delivery and learner access arrangements.
  • Risk register, action plan and continuous improvement records.
  • AI acceptable-use, authenticity and data protection guidance where relevant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers before your organisation starts centre development support.

These FAQs are written for organisations that are exploring training centre setup, approval readiness, curriculum development or quality assurance improvement.

Can you help if we are starting from scratch?

Yes. Support can begin with centre planning, delivery model design, staff role mapping, core documents, learner journey design and readiness actions.

Do you guarantee awarding organisation approval?

No. Approval decisions sit with the relevant awarding organisation. HHF Training can help your organisation prepare stronger systems, clearer documents and better evidence for approval readiness.

Can you review existing centre documents?

Yes. Existing policies, procedures, quality documents, curriculum plans, assessment tools and staff records can be reviewed for clarity, consistency and gaps.

Can you build assessment and IQA systems?

Yes. Support can include assessment strategy, evidence mapping, IQA sampling plans, standardisation records, assessor feedback templates and action tracking.

Can you train assessors, IQAs and centre staff?

Yes. Staff development can be delivered as workshops, CPD sessions, consultancy support or bespoke training linked to your centre systems.

Can this include AI policy and authentic assessment?

Yes. HHF Training can support AI acceptable-use guidance, AI assessment policy, staff AI training and practical strategies for protecting assessment authenticity.
